Our Mission

Say Goodbye to Phone Call Hassles & Double Bookings

Born in the heart of Phnom Penh, SportZone Arena was established by a passionate group of Cambodian athletes and technologists. For years, organizing friendly football matches or badminton sessions required endless phone calls, uncertain availability, and manual cash handovers.

We built SportZone Arena to give Cambodian sports enthusiasts the modern experience they deserve: transparent real-time calendars, automated peak and off-peak rates, and instant digital payments powered by the National Bank of Cambodia's Bakong KHQR standard.

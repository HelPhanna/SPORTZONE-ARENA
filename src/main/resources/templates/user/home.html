Everything you need to know about booking sports fields in Phnom Penh, instant Bakong KHQR payments, cancellations, and venue amenities.

Booking a sports pitch takes less than 60 seconds with our seamless real-time platform:

  1. Select a sport: Browse categories (Football, Futsal, Badminton, Tennis, Basketball, or Volleyball).
  2. Pick a field & date: Choose your preferred venue and view live available hourly time slots.
  3. Confirm details: Select an open slot, enter your player details, and tap Book Now.
  4. Pay with Bakong KHQR: Scan the instant KHQR code using any Cambodian bank app.
Instant Confirmation: Your booking is instantly confirmed in our live database, preventing any double-booking. Your digital pass and booking details are accessible anytime under My Bookings.

SportZone Arena is powered by the National Bank of Cambodia's official Bakong KHQR standard:

  • Universal Bank Compatibility: Scan the generated QR code using ABA Mobile, Wing Bank, ACLEDA mobile, Canadia Bank, Sathapana, TrueMoney, or the official Bakong app.
  • Zero Extra Fees: There are 0% hidden fees or transaction surcharges. You pay exactly the listed field rental price.
  • Automated Verification: No need to take screenshots or manually upload bank receipts. Our system verifies transaction settlement automatically in real-time.
100% Encrypted & Safe: All transactions are settled directly through Cambodia's secure interbank clearing network.

We understand team schedules can change. Here is our flexible policy:

  • Up to 12 Hours in Advance: You can cancel or request a date/time reschedule directly from your My Bookings profile without penalty.
  • Reschedule Vouchers: You will receive a venue booking credit or date-change confirmation for an alternative available time slot.
  • Late Notice (< 12 hours): For last-minute changes, please contact our Phnom Penh support hotline immediately so we can coordinate directly with the pitch master.

Cambodian tropical showers happen, and player safety always comes first:

  • Outdoor Pitches (Football & Open Tennis): If extreme downpours, waterlogging, or thunderstorm conditions render the pitch unplayable, the venue manager will issue a free Weather Reschedule Credit valid for 30 days.
  • Indoor Venues (Futsal, Badminton, Basketball): All indoor venues are fully sheltered, temperature-controlled, and operate normally rain or shine.
Tip: You can check weather forecasts on match day, or call the venue manager listed on your booking ticket for real-time turf status.
